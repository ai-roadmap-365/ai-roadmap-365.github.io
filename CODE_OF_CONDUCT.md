# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a place where people learn in public, which means being
visibly wrong sometimes. Make that safe for them.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Answering a beginner's question as though it were a good question, because
  it is
- Giving and gracefully accepting corrections — especially factual ones
- Focusing on what is best for people trying to learn
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Mocking someone for not knowing something
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project maintainer through a GitHub issue or direct message.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith
may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project's
leadership.
