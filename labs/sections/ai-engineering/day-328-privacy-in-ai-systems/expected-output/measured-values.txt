Every identifier in the fixtures is synthetic and invalid by construction:
example.com is a reserved domain (RFC 2606), 192.0.2.0/24 is the reserved
TEST-NET-1 range (RFC 5737), and the card number fails the Luhn check.

The line worth reading twice:

    INCOMPLETE deleted_from=2 still_present=audit_log,response_cache

Two deletes succeeded and the erasure was NOT fulfilled. Without the read-back
this run reports two successful deletions and looks complete.

A note from writing the tests rather than the code: an assertion that a token
does not contain "ada" FAILED against a correct redactor, because the SHA-256
digest happened to end in "ada" -- which is valid hexadecimal. A privacy test
that raises false alarms gets muted, and a muted test protects nothing. Assert
on the whole identifier and on fragments containing non-hex characters.
