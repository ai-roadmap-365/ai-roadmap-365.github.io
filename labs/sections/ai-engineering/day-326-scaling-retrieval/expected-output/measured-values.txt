Two things worth checking in your own run:

1. nprobe=8 costs 40160, which is MORE than the 40000 of exact search. The
   extra 160 are centroid comparisons -- 8 lists across 20 queries. An index
   tuned for perfect recall is strictly worse than no index.

2. The curve flattens after nprobe=4. The last four settings together buy 3
   points of recall for twice the work.
