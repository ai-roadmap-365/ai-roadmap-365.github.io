The four configurations, same six-request workload:

  configuration        total     per request   note
  no cache, full ctx   $0.0085   $0.00141      baseline
  cache, full ctx      $0.0079   $0.00131      2 hits, saved 7%
  cache, trimmed ctx   $0.0066   $0.00109      saved 22% against baseline
  tight budget         stopped   --            refused before overspending

The number worth staring at is the second row. The cache hit 2 of 6 requests --
a 33% hit rate -- and saved 7% of the cost, because the repeated question
routed to the SMALL model. Spend by model on the baseline:

  large  $0.0073   (86% of spend, from 2 of 6 requests)
  small  $0.0012

Hit rate is not savings. Trimming context saved more precisely because it acted
on the large-model calls.

Projection: $1,092 per million requests, from $0.00109 per request.
