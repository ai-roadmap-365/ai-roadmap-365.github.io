Before you implement the TODOs, the starter is expected to fail. Running

    bash tests/run_tests.sh

against an untouched starter/ingest.py produces:

    10 failed, 1 passed

The single pass is test_extract_raises_on_unreadable_payload, which exercises
extract() and does not depend on any of the stubbed functions. Every other test
fails with NotImplementedError until you complete content_hash, chunk_document
and run_once.
