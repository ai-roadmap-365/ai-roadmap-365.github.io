# Research Brief: Solid-State Batteries

## Executive Summary
This research investigation analyzed key findings regarding Solid-State Batteries. The primary findings are detailed below:

- Prototypes achieved 450 Wh/kg gravimetric density. [1]
- Cycle life reached 1200 cycles at C/3 charge rate. [2]
- Cell-level costs projected at $95/kWh by 2028. [3]
