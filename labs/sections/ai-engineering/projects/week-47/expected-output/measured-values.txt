Two documents are dead-lettered: one has no body at all, one is punctuation
that fails the alphabetic-ratio gate. Four identifiers are redacted -- and they
are redacted BEFORE the text is chunked and embedded, so no vector carries
them.

Cost for three questions, one of them a repeat:

  total=$0.00230
  large=$0.00210  small=$0.00017  retrieval=$0.00002  cache=$0.00000

Retrieval appears in the same ledger as generation. That is the point: a recall
setting is also a cost setting, and it only looks that way once the stages
share a budget.

Three stores, all verified by read-back. The cache is the one that gets
forgotten, and it is the one that can keep answering from deleted content.
